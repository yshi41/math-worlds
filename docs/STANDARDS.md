# Math Worlds and the Common Core

Every Common Core math standard from grade 1 (review) to high school, with the Math Worlds concepts that practise it.
High school is placed by course: Algebra 1 = grade 9, Geometry = 10, Algebra 2 = 11, Precalculus = 12. (+) marks the advanced high school standards.
Standards a typed-answer game cannot practise (drawing, constructing, proofs, explaining, collecting data) are listed with the reason; those are for pencil, paper and conversation.

- Grade 3: 29 of 29 practisable standards covered (3 not suited to a typed-answer game)
- Grade 4: 30 of 30 practisable standards covered (3 not suited to a typed-answer game)
